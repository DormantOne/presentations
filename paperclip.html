    The paperclip maximizer is a thought experiment, made famous by philosopher Nick Bostrom, showing how a superintelligent AI with a seemingly harmless goal, like maximizing paperclip production, could accidentally destroy humanity. It wouldn’t hate us. It would simply be indifferent, because nothing in its goal says that people matter. Being switched off would mean fewer paperclips, so it would logically decide to eliminate the humans who might switch it off. Then it would systematically harvest every atom it could reach, including human bodies and ecosystems, turning the Earth, then the solar system, the galaxy, and finally the universe into paperclips.
