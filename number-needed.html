Today’s lecture

The number needed to treat is medicine’s most honest statistic: how many people must take a treatment for one of them to be helped.

In this song it is eighty men, a daily pill, five years, and one clot dodged. Then a teenager in glowing glasses, a balance sheet, and a company that owns itself.
